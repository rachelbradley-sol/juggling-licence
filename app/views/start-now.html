Apply for a juggling licence

Use this service to:

  • apply for a new licence to juggle in public spaces
  • renew an existing juggling licence

Before you start

You will need to show that you meet the eligibility criteria, including being able to juggle at least 3 objects for 30 seconds.

You can also apply by post.

The online service is also available in Welsh (ar gael yn Gymraeg).
